Company Overview

ReyaAga Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd is a multidisciplinary engineering consulting firm established in 2026, delivering innovative engineering solutions in transportation, water, sanitation, housing and urban development. Our consulting firm ReyaAga Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd, focuses on integrated results designed for improving the performance and competence of our entity. Our team comprises knowledgeable, skilled, and resolute personnel who are committed to offering sustainable engineering services and solutions by utilizing industry practices. The company operates through three core departments with six areas of expertise that will assist us in completing the township development of Riverfields, such as planning, overseeing site investigation and managing the construction process.

Background & History

Founded in Johannesburg, ReyaAga Consulting Engineers was created to provide sustainable infrastructure solutions that support economic growth and community development.
